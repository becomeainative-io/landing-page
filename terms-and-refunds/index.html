Terms and refunds

These terms apply when you buy a place in the Become AI-Native intensive. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What you buy

A place in one cohort of Become AI-Native, a 30-day intensive for managers. The checkout page and your invoice show your cohort and its start date.

The intensive has 14 lessons and 3 bonus lessons, about 30 hours in total. Each lesson has 20–30 minutes of theory, about 40 minutes of practice and a 20-minute test. There are weekly live sessions, and we record them. Each lab runs on a sandbox company, so you do not need access to the systems of your company. You have access to the materials and the recordings for 2 months.

What you need

A paid Claude plan with Claude Code (from $20 a month). It is not included in the price. You also need a Windows or macOS computer with administrator rights. You do not need to code.

Price and payment

The checkout page shows the price. VAT is not included: the checkout adds VAT for your country, if VAT applies.

You pay through Stripe: by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. A company can also pay by SEPA bank transfer. After the payment, you get a receipt and an invoice by email. If you give the VAT number of your company at the checkout, the invoice shows it.

Promotion codes

Sometimes we give promotion codes. A code gives the discount that we name when we send it. Each code is personal, works one time and has an end date. After the end date, the full price applies. A code cannot be combined with other discounts, and it cannot be exchanged for money.

After the payment

Within 24 hours, we send you a welcome email: the start date, what to prepare and how to get access to the intensive on our platform.

Your access is personal. Do not share your login, the materials or the recordings with other people.

Refunds

If the intensive is not for you, get a full refund until the end of week 2 of your cohort: the second Sunday after the start date. This applies also if you did not start the intensive. You do not need to give a reason.

To ask for a refund, write to lana@becomeainative.io from the email address that you used for the payment. We refund the full amount to the same payment method within 14 days of your message. Your bank can take a few more days. When we refund you, your access to the intensive ends.

After the end of week 2, we do not give refunds.

Your legal right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU, the law gives you 14 days to withdraw from the purchase. Our refund guarantee does not limit this right. If your 14 days end later than our guarantee, you keep your 14 days.

If we change the dates

If we move the start of your cohort or cancel it, we tell you by email. You then choose: a place in a later cohort, or a full refund.
