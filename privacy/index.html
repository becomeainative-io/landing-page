Privacy policy

This page explains what data becomeainative.io collects, why, and who processes it. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Become AI-Native is a trade name, based in Utrecht, the Netherlands. We decide how your data is used (we are the data controller). For any question about your data, write to Lana (see Contact below).

Application and Black Friday form

On our main page, you can apply for a cohort. From 27 November to 12 December 2026, the same form also gives you a personal Black Friday code. The form asks for your name, your email address, your LinkedIn profile link, your role, your work situation and the one thing that you want to change in how you work.

If you open the page from a link that we sent you, for example on LinkedIn or by email, the link can also give us your LinkedIn ID (linkedin-id) and the place where you found the link (source).

We use this data to review your application, to make your personal code, and to send you the code, reminders until 12 December 2026 and emails about the intensive. To stop the emails, reply "stop" to any of them.

If you stop before the end of the form, Tally saves the answers that you already gave.

Quiz

The quiz at form.becomeainative.io/quiz asks about a normal work week and how you use AI. It does not ask for your company name.

At the end, you can give your first name and your email address. Both are optional. If you give your email address and agree, we send you your result, your personal plan, a personal Black Friday code and related emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.

If you open the quiz from a link that we sent you, the link can include your LinkedIn ID and the place where you found the link.

Payment

You pay on a payment page of Stripe. Stripe gets your card details, your name, your email address, your billing address and, if you give it, the VAT number of your company. We do not see or keep your card data.

From Stripe, we get your name, your email address, your billing address, the amount, the code that you used and, if the link had it, your LinkedIn ID. We use this data to give you access to the intensive, to send receipts and invoices, and for our accounting.

Research survey

In October and November 2026, we ran a short research survey: "How do experienced managers really use AI?"

The survey asks about your role, your experience, the size of your company and how you use AI at work. In some questions, you can type your own answer. The survey does not ask for your name or the name of your company.

At the end of the survey, you can give your email address, so that we can send you the results. This question is optional. We use your email address to send you the results of this research. We can also send you emails about our programs and offers. You can ask us at any time to stop sending you emails.

If you open the survey from a link that we sent you on LinkedIn, the link can include your LinkedIn ID. We use your LinkedIn ID only to connect your answers to our LinkedIn conversation with you.

If you stop before the end of the survey, Tally saves the answers that you gave.

We use the answers only for this research. We publish only total results. We do not publish or share personal names or company names.

LinkedIn conversations

If we talk with you on LinkedIn, we keep the public data of your LinkedIn profile (for example your name, headline, company and location) and our conversation. We keep them in Kanbox, a tool for LinkedIn messages, and in our internal files. We use them to reply to you and to send you messages about our research and the intensive. To stop the messages, tell us on LinkedIn or write to Lana.

Services that process your data

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We share it only with these services, which process it for us:

  • Tally: the forms, the quiz and the research survey. Tally stores your answers and passes them to us. The forms on our pages load in an embedded frame from tally.so.
  • Stripe: the payments, the personal codes, the receipts and the invoices.
  • n8n: the automation that makes your personal code and sends our emails.
  • Google Workspace: the email account that sends our emails and keeps our replies.
  • Kanbox: our LinkedIn messages.
  • Google Analytics: statistics about the site, only if you accept (see below).
  • GitHub Pages: the hosting of this site (see below).

Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, for example which pages they open, which buttons they click (such as the quiz button) and how many people send the form. It sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to two years.

We do not send your name, your email address or your LinkedIn ID to Google Analytics. If a page address has your LinkedIn ID, we hide the ID before Google Analytics gets the address.

Google Analytics is only loaded if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you choose, or if you decline, your browser makes no requests to Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept and later change your mind, we stop loading Google Analytics and remove its cookies. When it is loaded, data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and keep it secure. Our own fonts and scripts are hosted on this site. The only external services that load on our pages are Tally, on pages that show a form, and Google Analytics, if you accept it. The payment page opens on stripe.com.

How long we keep it

We keep application details until the cohort is selected and the program starts, or until you ask us to delete them.

We keep your quiz answers, your personal code and your email address until you ask us to delete them.

We keep payment records and invoices for 7 years, because Dutch tax law tells us to.

We keep research survey answers only while we need them for this research. Then we delete them. You can ask us to delete your answers at any time. The published total results do not contain personal data, so they can stay published.

We keep LinkedIn conversations until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or to the data protection authority of your country.

Contact

Write to Lana at lana@becomeainative.io with any privacy question or request.
