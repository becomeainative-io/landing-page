Privacy policy

This page explains what data __DOMAIN__ collects, why, and who processes it. Last updated: 12 October 2026.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, email address and, if you fill it in, what you'd like to learn. We use this only to contact you about the launch. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Web3Forms

The waitlist form is delivered by Web3Forms, which receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Web3Forms processes the data only to deliver it to us.

Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how many people join the waitlist. It sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to two years.

Google Analytics is only loaded if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you choose, or if you decline, your browser makes no requests to Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept and later change your mind, we stop loading Google Analytics and remove its cookies. When it is loaded, data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties. The only external services are Web3Forms, when you submit the waitlist form, and Google Analytics, if you accept it.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until launch, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
