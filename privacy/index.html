Privacy

Privacy policy

This page explains what data becomeainative.io collects, why, and who processes it. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What we collect

When you apply for a cohort, we collect your name, email address, your role and current work situation, and, if you fill them in, your LinkedIn profile link and what you want to change in how you work. We use this only to review your application and contact you about the program. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Black Friday code

From 24 November to 12 December 2026, you can get a personal Black Friday code with the form on our main page. For this, we collect your first name and your email address. If you open the page from a link that we sent you, for example on LinkedIn, the link can also give us your LinkedIn ID (linkedin-id) and the place where you found the link (source).

We use this data to send you the code, reminders until 12 December 2026 and emails about the intensive. We do not sell your data or share it with other companies. To stop the emails, reply "stop" to any of them.

Research survey

In October and November 2026, we run a short research survey: "How do experienced managers really use AI?"

The survey asks about your role, your experience, the size of your company and how you use AI at work. In some questions, you can type your own answer. The survey does not ask for your name or the name of your company.

At the end of the survey, you can give your email address, so that we can send you the results. This question is optional. We use your email address to send you the results of this research. We can also send you emails about our programs and offers. You can ask us at any time to stop sending you emails. We do not sell your email address or share it with other companies.

If you open the survey from a link that we sent you on LinkedIn, the link can include your LinkedIn ID. We use your LinkedIn ID only to connect your answers to our LinkedIn conversation with you.

If you stop before the end of the survey, Tally saves the answers that you gave.

We use the answers only for this research. We publish only total results. We do not publish or share personal names or company names. We do not sell the answers or use them for advertising.

Tally

The application form and the Black Friday code form are provided by Tally and is shown on our pages in an embedded frame loaded from tally.so. The research survey is also provided by Tally and opens on a Tally page. Tally stores your answers and passes them to us. When a form loads, your browser connects to Tally, which receives your IP address and basic request details, as any website does.

Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how many people apply for a cohort. It sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to two years.

Google Analytics is only loaded if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you choose, or if you decline, your browser makes no requests to Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept and later change your mind, we stop loading Google Analytics and remove its cookies. When it is loaded, data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and keep it secure. Our own fonts and scripts are hosted on this site. The only external services are Tally, which loads the application form on pages that show it, and Google Analytics, if you accept it.

How long we keep it

We keep application details until the cohort is selected and the program starts, or until you ask us to delete them.

We keep research survey answers only while we need them for this research. Then we delete them. We keep your email address until you ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to delete your answers at any time. The published total results do not contain personal data, so they can stay published.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
