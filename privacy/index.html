Privacy

Privacy policy

This page explains what data becomeainative.io collects, why, and who processes it. Last updated: 12 October 2026.

What we collect

When you apply for the Cohort 1 waitlist, we collect your name, email address, your role and current work situation, and, if you fill them in, your LinkedIn profile link and what you want to change in how you work. We use this only to review your application and contact you about the program. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Web3Forms

The waitlist form is delivered by Web3Forms, which receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Web3Forms processes the data only to deliver it to us.

Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how many people join the waitlist. It sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to two years.

Google Analytics is only loaded if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you choose, or if you decline, your browser makes no requests to Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept and later change your mind, we stop loading Google Analytics and remove its cookies. When it is loaded, data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so we don't ask again. You can change it at any time:

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and keep it secure. Fonts are hosted on this site; we don't load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties. The only external services are Web3Forms, when you submit the waitlist form, and Google Analytics, if you accept it.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and application details until the cohort is selected and the program starts, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Contact

Email Connect on LinkedIn with any privacy question or request.
