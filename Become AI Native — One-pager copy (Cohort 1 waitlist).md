## 2. The problem

**The PM role didn't disappear. It merged.**

- Your company says "AI-first". Nobody explained what that means for your job.
- Job posts ask for AI experience you can't prove yet.
- You use ChatGPT daily — but your real work still runs like it did in 2020.

In January 2026, LinkedIn replaced its Associate Product Manager program with "Associate Product Builder": one person who owns the product from idea to launch, with AI doing the legwork.

> "My company told us 70% of our work has to go through AI. Effective immediately. It's in my OKRs." — a product owner we spoke to

## 3. The solution

**AI-native = Mindset + Efficiency**

**Mindset:** knowledge lives in documents AI can read, not in people's heads. You ask AI first. You stay the owner.

**Efficiency:** your work tools are connected to AI, and anything you repeat gets automated.

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| "The person who knows our strategy is busy for two months." | Ask the knowledge base. Answer in 15 seconds. |
| Status updates built by hand from five tools | AI drafts them; you review and send |

## 4. How it works

**Three pillars:**

1. **Mindset** — how to think and work when AI is your first teammate.
2. **Tools** — connect Jira, Confluence, Slack and your docs to AI. No code.
3. **Method** — run delivery from idea to shipped work, the AI-native way.

**You leave with:** your own AI knowledge base, connected tools and automations running on your real work.

**Format:** recorded modules + weekly live sessions with the team, inside the live AI setup of WinWin.travel.
