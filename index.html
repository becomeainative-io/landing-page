BAI-03 · The world has changed

AI changed engineering, design and marketing. Management is no exception.

If you think management hasn't changed, you're wrong. As AI takes on routine work, the boundaries between management roles are shifting.

MANAGEMENT · ROLE DESCRIPTION · REVISED

Engineering management, project management, product, delivery and business analysis: responsibilities that used to sit in separate roles are coming together.

That does not mean every role becomes one. It means one person can take on responsibilities that previously required two, three or four roles, with AI handling much of the routine.

new rules. new way of working.

This demands a new way of working. We teach you the methodology, tools and mindset to work across those boundaries and deliver results.
