BEFORE YOU START
I am not technical. Do I need to code?

No. You need no coding experience. If you can write a good Jira ticket, you can do this. You give instructions to the agent in plain language. We show each click: install, connect, first task.

What do I need on my computer?

A Windows or macOS computer with administrator rights, and a paid Claude plan with Claude Code (from $20 a month). The plan is not in the price of the intensive.

My company does not permit AI tools yet. Can I still take part?

Yes. Each lab has a sandbox company with its own tasks, documents and tools. You do all the practice there, on your own computer. When your company permits AI tools, you move the setup to your real work.

I already use ChatGPT or Copilot every day. What is new for me?

A chat window answers one question at a time, and you copy the context into it. An AI agent works in your tools: it reads the tickets, the specs and the Slack threads, and it does a task from start to end. In our research, 78% of the managers who already use AI agents got no training. The intensive gives the method: context, skills, review and ownership.

Our team uses other tools, not Jira and Confluence. Does it still work?

Yes. We teach the method on Jira, Confluence and Slack, and you connect the agent to the tools that your team uses: for example Notion, Linear, Google Drive or GitLab. You do not change platforms.

You learn to give the agent the right context, to set clear yes/no criteria and to review the result before it goes anywhere. The agent does not act without your approval. Ownership and accountability stay with you. This is the "Own the results" part of the program.
